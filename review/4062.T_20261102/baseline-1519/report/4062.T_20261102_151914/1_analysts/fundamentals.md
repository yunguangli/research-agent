# Fundamental Analysis: 4062.T

**Note on fiscal labels:** IBIDEN labels the year ending March 2027 "FY2026". Throughout this report I use the model's convention — FY3/27 = year to March 2027, FY3/28 = year to March 2028 — and treat "FY2027" in the internal notes as FY3/28, except where the notes' meaning is stated explicitly.

## 1. Executive conclusion

- **Fundamental rating: Neutral** (risk/reward negative-skewed). Decision horizon: 3–6 months.
- **Business direction: strongly positive.** 4062.T is the top supplier of high-end IC package substrates and is running at effectively sold-out capacity in a structural shortage it expects to persist to CY2030 [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-company-presentation-20261030 p.12].
- **Earnings direction: positive but already heavily pre-paid in the share price.** H1 FY3/27 operating profit of ¥60.1bn was a record [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.1]; the FY3/27 model revenue of 565.0 and operating profit of 133.0 are exactly management guidance.
- **Forecast position versus guidance/consensus:** the model matches FY3/27 guidance by construction and matches FY3/28 consensus OP of 190.0, but sits **above management's FY3/28 target of ¥165bn** and above management's FY3/28 revenue of ¥650bn (model 665.0). Reversing management's disclosed ~¥10bn price-decline buffer justifies ~¥175bn; the extra to 190.0 requires Cell 8 and mix upside that is not yet evidenced.
- **Cash-flow quality: weak underneath the headline.** H1 operating cash flow of ¥193.2bn includes a ¥118.8bn increase in customer advances [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.9, p.5]. Excluding that, underlying cash generation was roughly ¥74bn against capex that is guided to ¥350bn in FY3/28 and above ¥400bn from FY3/29 [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call].
- **Balance sheet: strong.** Capital adequacy 53.5%, and net cash of roughly ¥258bn after customer prepayments [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.1, p.4–5].
- **Valuation: expensive.** At 8,045 the stock is 51.2x FY3/27 EPS of 157.2 and about 37x FY3/28 model EPS of 215.4. The model's fair-value band is 5,501–7,858 with a base of 7,539 (−6%), and my own sensitivity on a lower FY3/28 OP (¥175bn) gives roughly ¥6,600–7,200.
- **Main catalyst (in horizon):** Q3 FY3/27 results (late January 2027) and the Cell 7/Cell 9 investment decision plus any medium-term-plan revision management has promised by end-FY3/27.
- **Main risk:** FY3/28 guidance of ¥165bn is below consensus of ¥190bn, so the more likely near-term revision direction for consensus is *down*, not up, while the stock carries a ~37x forward multiple.
- **Principal limitation:** no revenue, net profit or EPS consensus is available anywhere in the corpus; only one FY3/28 OP consensus figure exists, cited second-hand by Citi [doc-4062.T-brokers-views-oct-30-2026].

## 2. Company and earnings structure

4062.T makes money in three reported segments (H1 FY3/27, external sales / segment profit):

| Segment | H1 sales ¥bn | H1 segment profit ¥bn | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electronics (IC package substrates) | 172.5 | 50.0 | 29.0% |
| Ceramics (DPF, AFP, NEB, FGM) | 46.2 | 5.4 | 11.7% |
| Others | 43.8 | 4.6 | 10.5% |
| Consolidated | 262.5 | 60.1 | 22.9% |

Source: [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.12]. Electronics is ~83% of H1 operating profit and is the entire investment case; Ceramics and Others are ballast.

**How Electronics earns:** semi-additive-process (SAP) build-up substrates for AI GPUs, AI ASICs, server CPUs and high-end switches. The economics are driven by *substrate area and layer count*, not unit volume: management expects GPU/ASIC units to grow 1.8x from CY26 to CY30 but required SAP demand to grow 5.4x, and server CPU units 1.3x against 2.5x area demand [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-company-presentation-20261030 p.12]. Larger dies (3.5 reticle in CY25 to 9+ by CY30) and more layers (SAP 9-X-9 to 14-X-14) raise price per unit and equipment time per substrate [same, p.14].

**Barriers and position:** 4062.T describes itself as top supplier in the high-end market [p.12]. The real constraint is qualified capacity — equipment lead times for lasers, laminators and plating tools, plus support engineers — which management calls the binding bottleneck, not construction [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call, Q3 and Q5]. Yields at tier-2 suppliers are described as very low, which limits how much nominal competitor capacity is real.

**Concentration:** not disclosed in the corpus. Two indirect signals of high customer concentration are visible: (i) ¥199.7bn of "advances received" on the balance sheet at 30 September 2026, up ¥118.8bn in six months [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.5, p.9] — customer prepayments for capacity; and (ii) capital investment explicitly justified by "agreements with our customers" [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call]. This is strong demand validation but also a single-customer dependency that is not quantified.

**Competition:** management confirms AI GPU substrates are moving from a two-player to a three-player structure with a Korean entrant, and that CPUs are contested by 3–4 suppliers; its offsetting argument is that aggregate competitor capacity remains far below demand [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call, Q1]. Citi separately flags that 4062.T's own high-end capacity grows ~2x while market area demand grows 3.5x — i.e. share erosion is arithmetically implied even in a tight market [doc-4062.T-brokers-views-oct-30-2026].

## 3. Latest results and operating drivers

H1 FY3/27 (six months to 30 September 2026), company-reported [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.1, p.7, p.12]:

- Net sales ¥262,546mn, +34.3% YoY; operating profit ¥60,116mn, +84.6%; ordinary profit ¥61,009mn, +88.9%; profit attributable to owners of parent ¥38,574mn, +74.8%.
- Gross profit ¥97,705mn (37.2% margin) versus ¥67,913mn (34.7%) a year earlier. SG&A rose only 6.4% to ¥37,589mn against 34.3% sales growth — this operating leverage, not gross margin, is the main source of the profit step-up.
- Basic EPS ¥68.63, diluted ¥65.10, both restated for the two 2-for-1 splits (1 January 2026 and 1 October 2026) [p.1].

**Company-confirmed drivers:** "favorable pricing and exceptionally strong demand for AI-related products" in Electronics; Ceramics slightly above plan on backup DPF part orders; Others in line [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call]. Q2 alone (H1 minus Q1) was revenue ¥139.3bn, OP ¥33.2bn, net ¥20.7bn, with OP margin up 2.0ppt sequentially to 23.8% [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy2027-results-forecast].

**Below the operating line — this matters for the net-profit comparison:** H1 non-operating income of ¥893mn included ¥675mn FX gains and ¥1,850mn interest income, but also ¥1,669mn of depreciation on inactive assets booked as non-operating. Extraordinary items were a net ¥1,650mn loss (¥813mn subsidy income offset by an equal loss on reduction of non-current assets, plus ¥1,606mn retirement losses). Effective tax rate was 34.5% (¥20,490mn on ¥59,358mn pretax) [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.7].

**Why FY3/26 net profit (63.7) exceeded FY3/26 operating profit (62.0):** not a data error. FY3/26 pretax income was ¥91.1bn against ¥62.0bn of operating profit, driven by roughly ¥40.3bn of gains on investment securities (the FY3/26 cash-flow statement carries a ¥40.3bn non-cash deduction for that gain, and proceeds from investment sales of ¥57.4bn) [vendor cash-flow statement, FY ending 2026-03-31; doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.9 for the analogous line]. The model's −28 "FY3/26 one-off gain reversal" in the net-profit bridge is therefore well supported, not an arbitrary plug.

**Cash-flow caveat on the result:** the ¥193.2bn H1 operating cash flow is dominated by the ¥118.8bn advance inflow and ¥11.2bn payables increase; inventory absorbed ¥12.0bn and receivables ¥9.3bn [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.9]. This was an excellent *reported* cash period and a much more ordinary *underlying* one.

## 5. Independent forecast

Model annual rows (quoted, not recalculated):

| ¥bn | FY3/26A | FY3/27E | FY3/28E | FY3/29E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 416.2 | 565.0 | 665.0 | 800.0 |
| Operating profit | 62.0 | 133.0 | 190.0 | 220.0 |
| Margin | 14.9% | 23.5% | 28.6% | 27.5% |
| Net profit | 63.7 | 89.0 | 122.0 | 145.0 |
| EPS (¥) | 112.5 | 157.2 | 215.4 | 256.0 |

Quarterly FY3/27 build: Q1 123.2/26.9/17.9, Q2 139.3/33.2/20.7, Q3 141.2/34.0/23.5, Q4 161.2/38.8/26.9 (revenue/OP/net profit).

**My assessment by year:**

- **FY3/27 — accept guidance.** H1 delivered ¥60.1bn; H2 needs ¥72.9bn. The model's H2 quarterly split (revenue 141.2 then 161.2, OP 34.0 then 38.8) implies a Q4 margin of about 24.1%, essentially flat on Q2. Given that H1 sequential margin improvement was already 2.0ppt, I regard this as slightly conservative — a modest beat to ¥136–140bn OP is plausible. I flag this as a *possible* upside but do not adopt it, because (a) the model's Q3/Q4 revenue split borrows FY3/26 seasonality (46.7%/53.3%) [model warning] rather than any company disclosure, and (b) H2 capex is heavily back-end loaded (only ¥48.3bn of PP&E purchases in H1 against ~¥200bn guided for the year), which typically brings startup cost and depreciation noise.
- **FY3/28 — I am below the model.** The model at 190.0 requires group operating margin of 28.6%, above management's 25.4%. The chain is: reverse the ¥10bn price buffer → ~¥175bn; add Cell 8 first contribution and mix → up to ¥190bn. The first step is broker-corroborated; the second is not. The model also assumes revenue of 665.0 versus management's ¥650bn, a +2.3% volume/mix gap for which I find no supporting disclosure — and Cell 8 is described as ramping only *sequentially from FY2027* (i.e. FY3/28), with the "main operational ramp-up and corresponding depreciation impact" occurring "from FY2028 onward," which in company speech means FY3/29 [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call, Q5]. My base case is **FY3/28 OP ¥175–185bn on ¥650–665bn revenue**, centring near ¥180bn.
- **FY3/29 — treat as an extrapolation, not a forecast.** The model's 220.0 is a linearisation between FY3/28 and the FY3/31 target. Cell 8 ramp +80 is an assumption; Cell 6 (¥151bn, first contribution FY2030) and Cell 7/9 (mass production beyond FY2030) do not belong in FY3/29. I would not underwrite 220.0.

**Where the verified model is weak on method** (Section 7 obligation): the FY3/27 revenue bridge adds +95 "Electronics AI-substrate volume" derived by applying *total company* H1 growth of +34.3% to the FY3/26 base, and then separately adds +30 of "pricing/mix." Total-company growth already contains Electronics price and mix, so these two lines risk double counting. Electronics H1 revenue actually grew 52%, not 34.3%, so the derived volume line is also mis-specified at the segment level. The bridge still sums to revenue, but the *attribution* between volume and price is not reliable and should not be used to argue incremental-margin economics. The same caution applies to the +45 volume/utilisation term in the FY3/27→FY3/28 OP bridge, which applies a ~45% incremental margin to revenue that is partly price.

## 6. Forecast bridges

**Revenue FY3/26 → FY3/27** (model): base 416.2, +95 Electronics AI-substrate volume [derived], +30 pricing/mix [assumption], +15 Ceramics/other recovery [assumption], +8.8 FX and rounding [derived] = 565.0. The Ceramics/other +15 is supported at the H1 level only (Ceramics OP ¥5.4bn, +40% YoY) and management guides Ceramics H2 OP down to ¥2.1bn on automotive weakness, so the full-year Ceramics contribution is smaller than the first half suggests. The +8.8 residual is small enough to be rounding/FX, but it is a residual, not a driver.

**Operating profit FY3/26 → FY3/27** (model): base 62.0, +50 volume/utilisation [derived], +18 pricing/mix net [assumption], −8 depreciation/startup [assumption], +6 Ceramics/other [assumption], +5 SG&A/other [derived] = 133.0. This is a defensible decomposition: the volume term reconciles to the observed H1 OP delta (+¥27.5bn YoY) plus H2 guidance (+¥43.5bn), and the −8 depreciation headwind is directly supported by the ¥81.0bn FY3/27 depreciation guide (versus ¥66.2bn in FY3/26 per the vendor cash-flow statement). The +5 SG&A residual is genuinely positive operating leverage — H1 SG&A grew 6.4% against 34.3% revenue growth — so labelling it a residual understates its quality, but it is a residual by construction.

**Operating profit FY3/27 → FY3/28** (model): base 133.0, +45 volume/utilisation [derived], +20 pricing/mix net of decline [derived], −12 depreciation/startup [assumption], +10 Cell 8 contribution [derived], −6 SG&A/other [derived] = 190.0. Two things concern me here. First, the +10 Cell 8 term applies a 65% incremental margin to a +15 revenue contribution — a margin assumption with no company disclosure behind it; management has never given Cell 8 revenue or margin guidance. Second, the −12 depreciation charge looks light: management guides depreciation up ¥9bn (to ¥90bn) while capex runs at ¥350bn; the model's own warning acknowledges depreciation rising 81→90 plus startup costs, yet only charges 12. Net effect: I would remove roughly half the Cell 8 term and add 3–5bn of startup/depreciation, which takes 190.0 to about ¥180bn.

**Net profit FY3/27 → FY3/28** (model): 89.0 + 57.0 OP increase −19 tax −5 minority/other = 122.0. The ~33% tax rate is consistent with H1's 34.5% and FY3/27's implied ~34%. I accept this conversion.

**Operating profit → net income, FY3/27 sanity check:** OP ¥133.0bn + non-operating ~¥1.5bn = ordinary ¥134.5bn (company guidance) with net profit ¥89.0bn implies a 66.2% net/pretax conversion. H1 actual was 65.0% (38.6/59.4). The two tie within 1.2ppt, so the model's −10 tax/minority line, while oddly described as "incremental OP × ~14%," is not producing a wrong answer.

## 7. Consensus and variant perception

- **FY3/27:** the model equals guidance exactly. **No forecast edge exists.**
- **FY3/28 operating profit:** the model at 190.0 equals the Citi-cited consensus and is ¥25bn *above* management's ¥165bn. Say clearly why the model differs from guidance: management embeds roughly ¥10bn of future product price declines which the model reverses, and the model adds Cell 8 plus mix on top. My own view (¥175–185bn) differs from the model because the Cell 8 contribution is not yet evidenced and management's own FY3/28 revenue is ¥650bn, not the model's 665.0.
- **Where a genuine edge may exist, it is negative, not positive:** management's FY3/28 target of ¥165bn is *below* consensus of ¥190bn. Because management reaffirmed an unchanged FY3/31 target and announced no Cell 7/Cell 9 investment, the near-term path of least resistance for consensus FY3/28 and FY3/29 numbers is downward revision. This is the opposite of the usual "conservative guidance = upside" trade, and three of four brokers in the corpus read the briefing that way (SMBC "impression is negative"; Citi "we see this as negative"; JPM notes the share price fell sharply on a below-expectation plan revision) [doc-4062.T-brokers-views-oct-30-2026].
- **Evidence that would confirm the model's higher path:** Q3 FY3/27 OP at or above ¥36.5bn with Electronics margin ≥30%; an FY3/28 Electronics margin printed above 31%; Cell 8 qualification completed on schedule; a Cell 7/Cell 9 announcement with customer-backed terms.
- **Evidence that would invalidate it:** any broad return of legacy price concessions; Cell 8 qualification slipping; a third AI-GPU supplier displacing incumbent allocation (Citi explicitly warns of a "temporary stock price correction" from share loss even with sales growth intact); EMIB-T yield stalling.

## 8. Cash flow and balance sheet

**Balance sheet (30 September 2026)** [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.4–6]:

- Total assets ¥1,127.6bn, up ¥167.2bn in six months. Total equity ¥610.9bn, capital adequacy 53.5%.
- Cash and deposits ¥420.5bn. Interest-bearing debt: current bonds ¥10.0bn, bonds payable ¥35.0bn, convertible-bond-type bonds ¥56.0bn, long-term loans ¥61.0bn, leases ¥0.05bn. **Net cash roughly ¥258bn** before the October CB conversion.
- **Advances received ¥199.7bn**, up from ¥81.0bn — 17.7% of total assets. This is the single most important non-obvious balance-sheet item: it is customer prepayment funding the capacity build, it inflates cash and operating cash flow, and it will unwind as revenue is recognised.
- Construction in progress ¥126.2bn (from ¥111.8bn). PP&E actually fell slightly (buildings and machinery both down on depreciation), confirming that the heavy spend is still work-in-progress, not yet in service — consistent with the "large portion remaining in Construction in Progress during FY2027" comment [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call, Q5].
- Subsequent event: ¥11.5bn face of the 2031 zero-coupon CB converted 1–21 October 2026, issuing 5,126,831 shares; remaining CB ~¥44.5bn [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.13]. Conversion price reset to ¥2,243.1 post-split [p.14], so the remaining CB is deep in the money and should be treated as equity.

**Cash flow:** H1 OCF ¥193.2bn; investing outflow ¥48.9bn (PP&E ¥48.3bn); financing outflow ¥18.3bn (¥15.0bn bond redemption, ¥4.2bn dividends). Cash rose ¥124.8bn.

**Free cash flow is the central analytical problem.** Capex guided ~¥200bn in FY3/27, ~¥350bn in FY3/28 and >¥400bn thereafter, against FY3/28 EBITDA that management guides toward a ~48% margin [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call]. On the model's 665.0 revenue, 48% EBITDA is roughly ¥319bn, giving EBITDA-capex of about −¥31bn before interest, tax and dividends. The internal note's simplified normalised FCF of −¥132bn for FY3/28 [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy2027-results-forecast] is in the right order of magnitude. **The dividend is not covered by free cash flow; the dividend is covered by earnings, cash balances and customer advances.** Payout ratio is ~14%/~9% of FY3/27/FY3/28 EPS, so it is small in absolute terms (yield ~0.25%).

**Funding:** net cash of ~¥258bn plus customer advances covers the FY3/28 capex step-up, but a ¥350–400bn annual capex run-rate with negative normalised FCF means the net cash position will erode quickly, and the company has repeatedly been a debt issuer in prior cycles (¥118.5bn and ¥120.0bn of debt issued in FY3/24 and FY3/23 respectively). Cell 7/Cell 9, if approved, would extend the negative-FCF period. No financial covenants are disclosed in the corpus — a genuine data gap.

## 9. Valuation

**Method:** P/E on FY3/28 EPS is the primary method, with EV/EBITDA as a cross-check. This is appropriate for a high-growth, asset-heavy manufacturer whose earnings are still ramping; DCF is not usable because the corpus lacks a discount rate, a working-capital profile and any disclosure of the terminal capex/return relationship.

**Share basis.** The model uses the full-share count of 566.3m implied by company FY3/27 EPS guidance [model warning]. Current actual basic shares are lower: 567,387,652 issued less 1,859,388 treasury = 565.5m net at 30 September; adding the 5.13m shares issued on CB conversion gives ~570.6m [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-semi-annual-tanshin p.3, p.13]. That is ~0.8% dilution the model does not capture. Fully diluted, including the ~¥44.5bn remaining CB at ¥2,243.1, is higher still (the internal note uses 592.0m fully diluted).

**Model valuation:** price 8,045, FY3/27 EPS 157.2, P/E 51.2x. Fair value band 5,501–7,858, base 7,539 (−6%), bear 5,562 (−31%), bull 12,360 (+54%). On FY3/28 EPS of 215.4 the stock is 37.3x.

**My valuation view.** On my FY3/28 operating profit of ¥175–185bn and ~570.6m shares, FY3/28 EPS is ¥200–210. Applying 33–37x — a defensible band for a quasi-monopoly growth asset with negative FCF and a share-loss threat — gives **¥6,600–7,800, centred around ¥7,200**. That is below the model's 7,539 and materially below the market price. The internal note independently lands on a ¥8,000 target at ~37x diluted EPS with a "Neutral/Hold" rating and an accumulation range of ¥7,000–7,500 [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy2027-results-forecast].

**What the price requires:** at ~37x forward earnings and ~16–17x EV/EBITDA with negative free cash flow, the market is already discounting a successful Cell 8 ramp, sustained premium pricing, and continued growth beyond FY3/28. There is no valuation cushion for a Cell 8 delay or a consensus downgrade toward management's ¥165bn.

**Vendor conflict flagged:** Bloomberg snapshot shows P/E 56.19x and gross margin 31.64% [get_fundamentals, 2026-11-02]. The P/E is on trailing FY3/26 EPS and the gross margin on FY3/26 (H1 FY3/27 gross margin was 37.2%); neither is a forward measure. Per the source hierarchy the verified model's forward valuation wins.

## 10. Catalysts within the decision horizon (to ~May 2027)

1. **Q3 FY3/27 results, late January 2027.** Tests whether the H2 24.1% margin guide is conservative. Threshold: Q3 OP ≥¥36.5bn and gross margin ≥38.5% would support the model's FY3/27 beat potential; Q3 OP below ¥33bn would be a clear miss signal.
2. **Cell 7 / Cell 9 investment decision and possible medium-term-plan revision, promised "within this fiscal year" (by March 2027).** Management has said the FY3/31 target may be revised once pending capex is finalised; Citi names this the next catalyst. Timing is explicitly uncertain — possibly at Q3, possibly at full-year results [doc-4062.T-brokers-views-oct-30-2026].
3. **FY3/27 full-year results and first FY3/28 company guidance, late April/early May 2027.** This is where the ¥165bn versus ¥190bn consensus gap gets resolved. It is at the very edge of the horizon.
4. **Cell 8 installation and qualification milestones.** Cell 8 (Ono, ¥280bn, AI GPU substrates) starts sequentially from FY2027 [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-company-presentation-20261030 p.13]. Any visible qualification slippage in the horizon would hit FY3/28 estimates and the multiple simultaneously.

Explicitly **not** near-term catalysts: EMIB-T mass production (scheduled H2 next fiscal year, i.e. Oct 2027–Mar 2028, beyond the horizon); Cell 6 revenue (first contribution FY2030); Cell 7/9 mass production (beyond FY2030, and management says a minimum four years from decision to mass production); glass-core substrates.

## 11. Risks and invalidation conditions

**Business risk.** Demand is not the risk; qualified capacity is. Two specific threats: (i) a third AI-GPU substrate supplier compressing 4062.T's allocation despite a tight market — Citi expects this to hit the multiple before it hits reported earnings; (ii) EMIB-T yields, where management openly says it is "struggling significantly" and which sits on the H2 FY3/28 mass-production schedule [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call, Q2/Q4].

**Forecast risk.** The model's 190.0 FY3/28 OP is ¥25bn above management. If management's conservative pricing assumption is *right* rather than conservative, FY3/28 lands at ¥165bn and consensus cuts ~13%.

**Balance-sheet / cash-flow risk.** Capex of ¥350bn in FY3/28 is below the >¥400bn level thereafter, against substantially negative normalised FCF. Funding is comfortable today (net cash ~¥258bn, 53.5% capital ratio) but the trajectory is dependent on continued customer advances. A "customer advance unwind" — if a major customer re-timed prepayments — would hit both reported operating cash flow and the funding plan at once.
