## 1. Strongest positive challenge

The note describes the area/layer mechanism in §2 and then does not carry it into the forecast. Management's own series — GPU/ASIC units 1.8x CY26→CY30 against required SAP demand 5.4x; server CPU units 1.3x against 2.5x area demand; die size from 3.5 reticle in CY25 to 9+ by CY30; layers from 9-X-9 to 14-X-14, with the binding constraint being qualified equipment hours rather than construction — describes revenue per constrained tool-hour rising structurally, not a cyclical price spike [doc-4062.T-ibiden-co-ltd-company-presentation-20261030 p.12, p.14]. That is a mix-driven uplift that survives unit-price deflation. It is visible in reported numbers: Electronics H1 margin 29.0% against a FY3/28 guide of 31%. Yet the model prices it as a single +¥20bn line in FY3/28 and then has group margin *falling* from 28.6% to 27.5% in FY3/29 despite the Cell 8 full-year ramp — an internal contradiction the note does not address.

Strongest earnings-revision mechanism: Q3 FY3/27 (late January 2027), inside the horizon. H2 guidance implies OP ¥72.9bn on revenue ¥302.5bn, a 24.1% margin only 0.3ppt above Q2's 23.8%, while H1's sequential margin gain was 2.0ppt. Management has already raised once (¥127bn/¥550bn to ¥133bn/¥565bn). A Q3 print at or above ¥36.5bn with Electronics margin ≥30% makes a second raise the default and lifts the FY3/28 base with no Cell 8 assumption at all.

Most credible rerating mechanism: a Cell 7/Cell 9 decision with customer funding, promised within FY3/27 (by March 2027). It can rerate rather than merely add earnings because the FY3/31 ¥1,000bn/¥300bn+ target explicitly excludes Cell 6, the unused Cell 8 space and Cell 7/9; an approved, customer-backed expansion converts the target from aspiration to floor. Note, though, that the model's own bull cannot be reached on earnings alone: ¥12,360 is 57.4x the ¥215.4 EPS it is struck against, so it is a re-rating case wearing an earnings label.

Evidence required within the horizon: Q3 OP ≥¥36.5bn; Electronics margin ≥30%; a Cell 7/9 disclosure naming customer funding; FY3/28 revenue guidance ≥¥650bn.

## 2. Strongest negative challenge

The most overestimated assumption is not Cell 8 — it is reversal of the ¥10bn price-decline buffer. That figure is a GS estimate quoted in a broker note [doc-4062.T-brokers-views-oct-30-2026], not company disclosure; management's own language is that pricing pressure is "inevitable" and that it does not assume current conditions roll forward [doc-4062.T-ibiden-q2-fy3-2027-result-call]. The FY3/28 bridge then adds +¥20bn "pricing/mix net of decline" on top of +¥45bn computed as 45% of the ¥100bn of incremental revenue — and that ¥100bn already contains the +¥20bn price and the +¥15bn Cell 8. Price is counted twice (~¥9bn) and Cell 8 twice (~¥7bn); the drivers sum to ¥196bn against a stated ¥190bn, closed by a −¥6bn plug. A bridge that closes on a plug decomposes a number, it does not evidence it — and the number was set equal to consensus.

I also disagree with the analyst's own add-back. The −¥12bn depreciation charge is ¥9bn of guided depreciation (¥81.0bn to ¥90.0bn) plus ¥3bn of startup. Charging a further ¥3–5bn assumes management under-guides a line that is among the least likely in a Japanese filing to be under-guided, while ¥350bn of FY3/28 capex sits largely in construction in progress (¥126.2bn at 30 September 2026, from ¥111.8bn) and depreciates with a lag. Stripping only the Cell 8 overstatement gives ¥180bn, not ¥175bn.

Clearest downgrade mechanism: management guides FY3/28 to ¥650bn/¥165bn; the model prints ¥665bn/¥190bn, +15.2% on OP and +2.3% on revenue against the company's own plan. If the buffer is accurate rather than conservative, OP is ¥165bn, net profit ≈¥105.3bn (33% tax on the ¥32bn delta from ¥133bn, less ¥5bn minority), EPS ≈¥184.5 on 570.6m shares, and ¥6,460 at the model's own 35x — 19.7% below ¥8,045 and below the note's ¥7,200 base.

Most material valuation and cash-flow risk is the funding arithmetic, not the cash-quality optics. FY3/28 capex ¥350bn against depreciation ¥90.0bn; at management's ~48% EBITDA margin target on ¥665bn revenue, EBITDA is ~¥319bn, so EBITDA less capex is ≈−¥31bn before tax, interest and the ¥20 DPS. Net cash of ~¥258bn covers that shortfall; it does not cover a sustained >¥400bn run-rate without new debt or further advances. The ¥199.7bn advance book is 57.1% of the FY3/28 programme — it is the funding, not a footnote. Dilution is also understated: ¥122.0bn on the internal note's 592.0m fully diluted shares is ¥206.1, 4.5% below the ¥215.4 used.

Confirming evidence: Q3 OP below ¥33bn; gross margin below 37% (H1 37.2%, and price declines hit gross margin before OP); FY3/28 guidance below ¥165bn; advances received at 31 March 2027 below ¥199.7bn; FY3/27 depreciation above ¥81.0bn.

On labelling: a model base of ¥7,539 is −6.3% and the analyst's own ¥7,200 is −10.5% against ¥8,045. A "Neutral" with a stated negative skew and fair value 6–10% below spot is an Underweight under a softer name.

## 3. Common assumptions both sides may be missing

Both treat the ¥10bn buffer as discrete and reversible; at ¥565bn of FY3/27 revenue a 2ppt deeper decline is ¥11bn — the same size as the entire disputed item — so neither side has tested the sensitivity.

Neither asks what the ¥199.7bn of advances contractually is. If refundable or qualification-contingent, the bull's "customer-funded" rerating weakens; if a non-refundable capacity reservation, the bear's "contingent liability" is overstated. The corpus discloses neither.

Both ignore Ceramics: H2 segment OP guided to ¥2.1bn against ¥5.4bn in H1, a ¥3.3bn headwind named in neither bridge.

Both carry the FY3/29 row as decision-relevant. The model's own warning dates Cell 6 revenue to FY3/29 while the company dates Cell 6 to FY2030, and EMIB-T mass production sits in H2 FY3/28 — Oct 2027 to Mar 2028 — beyond the horizon.

Neither allows that the 41.5% drawdown from ¥13,750 to ¥8,045 has already partly discounted the ¥165bn-versus-¥190bn gap.
